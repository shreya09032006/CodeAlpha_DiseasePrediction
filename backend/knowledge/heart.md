# Heart health diet and exercise
Source: American Heart Association; World Health Organization
- Build meals around vegetables, fruit, whole grains, legumes, nuts and, if non-vegetarian, fish.
- Limit saturated fat, trans fat, processed meat, added sugar and fried snacks.
- Avoid trans fats such as partially hydrogenated oils and vanaspati.
- Use small amounts of unsaturated oils instead of large amounts of ghee, butter or palm oil.
- Keep salt below 5 grams (about one teaspoon) per day, including salt in pickles, papad and packaged foods.
- Fatty fish about twice a week is recommended if you eat fish.
- Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate or 75 minutes of vigorous activity per week, plus muscle-strengthening on 2 days.
- Stop smoking and tobacco use and limit alcohol.
- Chest pain, severe breathlessness or fainting needs emergency care. Do not exercise through these symptoms.
- People with known heart disease should speak to their doctor before starting a new exercise programme.
