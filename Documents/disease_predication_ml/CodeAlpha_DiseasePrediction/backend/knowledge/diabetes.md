# Diabetes diet and exercise
Source: American Diabetes Association Standards of Care; ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians
- Eat at regular times and avoid long gaps between meals, especially if taking glucose-lowering medicine.
- Prefer whole grains, millets, dal, beans, vegetables and fruit over refined flour, large portions of white rice and sweets.
- Limit added sugar, sweets, fruit juice and sugary drinks.
- Plate method: fill half the plate with non-starchy vegetables, a quarter with protein, and a quarter with whole grains.
- High-fibre foods such as dal, beans, oats and vegetables slow the rise in blood sugar.
- Limit alcohol and highly processed packaged foods.
- Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week, such as brisk walking or cycling, spread over at least 3 days.
- Do not go more than 2 days in a row without activity.
- Add resistance exercise on 2 to 3 non-consecutive days per week.
- If you take insulin or certain tablets, check blood sugar around exercise and carry a fast-acting sugar source.
- Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing medicines or starting a new exercise programme.
