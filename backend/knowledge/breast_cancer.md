# Breast health diet and exercise
Source: American Cancer Society guidelines on diet and physical activity
- Keep a healthy body weight through balanced meals and regular activity.
- Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity per week.
- Eat plenty of vegetables, fruit, whole grains and legumes.
- Limit processed and red meat, sugary drinks and highly processed foods.
- Alcohol raises breast cancer risk, so it is best to avoid it or keep it very low.
- Diet needs for people in cancer treatment are individual, so ask the oncology team or an oncology dietitian.
- Any new breast lump, nipple change or skin change should be checked by a doctor promptly.
